Purpose of This Assessment

This assessment is designed to understand your current Enterprise Architecture maturity and readiness for a SAP LeanIX implementation. It is not a test or a gate — there are no pass or fail outcomes.

SAP LeanIX and the Jumpstart methodology are built to support organisations at every maturity level, from those taking their first steps in EA to those with well-established practices. Whether your score is low or high, the Jumpstart is designed to meet you where you are and help you move forward.

The goal of this assessment is to:

  • Understand your current state so the implementation team can tailor the Jumpstart to your needs
  • Identify areas where preparation before kickoff will accelerate value delivery
  • Establish a maturity baseline that you can measure progress against over time
  • Ensure the right people, data, and systems are available for a smooth implementation

Every Maturity Level Is a Valid Starting Point

A low score does not mean you are not ready for LeanIX — it means the Jumpstart will be adapted to build the foundations with you. A high score means we can move faster and go deeper into advanced use cases from the start.

The Jumpstart is specifically designed to:

  • Low maturity: Establish your first EA practice, data baseline, and governance processes using LeanIX as the enabling platform
  • Moderate maturity: Operationalise use cases, close data gaps, and integrate LeanIX into existing IT processes
  • High maturity: Accelerate advanced use cases, automation, and strategic decision-making capabilities

How Scores Translate to Implementation Approach

Score RangeMaturity LevelWhat It Means for Your Jumpstart
1.0 – 2.0Starting OutThe Jumpstart will focus on establishing foundations: building your first application inventory, defining data ownership, and setting up governance. More time is allocated to data collection and stakeholder onboarding.
2.1 – 3.0BuildingCore EA building blocks are emerging. The Jumpstart will focus on formalising what exists, closing key gaps, and delivering your first actionable reports and dashboards.
3.1 – 4.0EstablishedStrong foundations in place. The Jumpstart can move quickly through setup and focus more time on advanced use cases, integrations, and stakeholder enablement.
4.1 – 5.0AdvancedMature EA practice. The Jumpstart focuses on maximising LeanIX capabilities, automation, advanced reporting, and embedding EA deeply into IT and business decision-making processes.

How to Complete This Assessment

  1. Go to the Scored Assessment tab and answer each question by selecting a score from 1 to 5 using the dropdown
  2. Use the Level 1, Level 3, and Level 5 descriptions as guidance for where you fall
  3. Add any context or comments in the Comments column — this helps the implementation team understand your situation
  4. View the Maturity Dashboard tab for a live summary of your scores and radar chart
  5. The Jumpstart Readiness section in the Scored Assessment includes all prerequisite items — score each one to track readiness
